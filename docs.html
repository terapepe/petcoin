Welcome to petcoin.fun

petcoin.fun is a meme coin launchpad where every coin starts with a real pet. Owners Memeify their dogs, cats and other animals, the community votes for the funniest moments with {ticker}, and the most-loved pets launch their own coins on a fair bonding curve.

The platform runs on {netName}. {ticker} ({name}) is the native token: it's used to stake votes, pay launch fees at a discount, and reward the owners of viral pets.

🐾 In one line: Upload your pet → the community votes → your pet gets a coin → you earn when it goes viral.

Why petcoin.fun?

  • Real pets, real owners. Coins are tied to verified pet owners, not stolen images.
  • Fair launch. Every pet coin starts on a bonding curve — no presale, no team allocation.
  • Owners get paid. 80% of every trading fee on a pet's coin goes to its creator (the pet owner).
  • Community-first. Voters who back winners share the Paw Pool.

📸 Memeify campaigns

A Memeify campaign is a 72-hour contest for one funny moment of your pet: a photo, a GIF or a short clip.

  1. Verify your pet once: connect your wallet, upload 3 photos of your pet and post a verification code on X. One pet = one owner wallet.
  2. Create the campaign: choose the moment, write a caption and pick a season theme. Fee: {campaignFee} (50% off in {ticker}).
  3. Share it: your campaign gets a page and a share card for X. Votes from your community count.
  4. Results: after 72h, campaigns are ranked by staked votes. Top 10 pets of each season get the fee refunded in {ticker}, a Hall-of-Fame badge and Paw Pool rewards.

Season themes

Zoomies Week · Derp Faces · Costume Contest · Sleepy Bois · Food Thieves · Bath Time Drama. New themes are voted by {ticker} holders.

Content rules: no animal harm, no stolen photos, no NSFW. Campaigns breaking the rules are removed and the fee is not refunded.

🗳️ Voting

Voting uses stake-to-vote: you lock {ticker} on a campaign for its duration. Your tokens are never spent — they unlock automatically when the campaign ends.

  • Minimum stake: 100 {ticker} per vote.
  • Vote weight = amount staked. One wallet can back several pets.
  • Voters who backed a Top 10 pet share the voter half of the Paw Pool, pro-rata to their stake.
  • Anti-bot: wallets must hold {ticker} for at least 24h before voting.

🪄 Launching a pet coin

Any verified pet can get its own coin. Campaign winners get a featured launch slot on the homepage.

  1. Open Launch, connect your wallet and upload your verified pet.
  2. Set name, ticker, image and links (X, Telegram, website).
  3. Choose the network ({netShort}) and a trading fee: 0.5%, 1%, 2%, 3% or 5% — the same rate for buys and sells. It cannot be raised after launch.
  4. Pay the creation fee: {launchFee} + gas. Pay in {ticker} for 50% off.
  5. Optional: make the first buy in the same transaction to avoid snipers.
  6. Your coin is live on the bonding curve instantly. Share the page!

📈 Bonding curve & graduation

Every pet coin starts on an automated market maker called a bonding curve. Price is a function of how many tokens have been sold: it rises as people buy and falls as they sell. There is no order book and no need for initial liquidity.

price = virtualReserve{native} ÷ virtualReservetoken
progress = marketCap ÷ {gradCap}

Graduation

When a coin's market cap reaches {gradCap}, it graduates:

  • The curve closes and the collected {native} plus the reserved 20% supply are added as liquidity on {dex}.
  • A graduation fee of {gradFee} is taken from curve liquidity.
  • The LP tokens are burned, so liquidity can never be pulled.
On the homepage, the progress bar on each token card shows market cap ÷ {gradCap}. Coins past the cap show 🎓.

🖼️ NFT Meme collections

The Top 10 pets of each season get a limited NFT Meme collection made from their winning moments.

  • Collections are minted on the pet's coin network.
  • {ticker} holders and winning voters get an early mint window.
  • Secondary royalty: 5% — 3% to the pet owner, 2% to the Paw Pool.
  • Part of every season's Paw Pool is donated to animal shelters.
